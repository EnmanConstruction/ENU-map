    About this data

    Active development counts City development-permit records currently marked In Progress.

    Housing growth counts new-building permits adding at least one home during the trailing 24 months.

    ENU presence is maintained by ENU. Unknown means the neighbourhood has not yet been confirmed—not that ENU is absent.

    Community leagues use City neighbourhood service geography where the relationship is clear. Hall locations come from the City’s Community League Halls dataset; unresolved neighbourhoods link to EFCL’s league finder.

    City permit snapshot date is loading. The status badge above shows whether the map is using its verified snapshot or a connected live sheet.
