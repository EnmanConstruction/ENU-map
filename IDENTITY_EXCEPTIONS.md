# Resident-facing place names

The public map leads with the name residents are most likely to use, while City neighbourhood and ward names remain visible as supporting civic geography.

## Curated aliases

- **Langdale** → City neighbourhood **Windermere**
- **Callingwood** → City neighbourhoods **Callingwood North** and **Callingwood South**
- **Riverbend** → City neighbourhoods **Brander Gardens, Brookside, Bulyea Heights, Ramsay Heights, and Rhatigan Ridge**

These mappings are intentionally explicit. The map does not guess aliases from proximity.
